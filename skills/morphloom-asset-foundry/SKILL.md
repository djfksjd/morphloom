# Morphloom Asset Foundry

Treat a short modeling instruction as a complete statement of ordinary quality expectations. Classify every applicable domain—including electronics, animation, games, 3D printing, and physical surface recovery—and apply all of their delivery gates without asking the user to repeat standard requirements, but do not treat one image or any fixed file count as the workflow target. Morphloom targets semi-professional editable assets from whatever combination of drawings, dimensions, datasheets, scans, existing CAD, and photographs resolves the required properties. Infer ordinary expectations about likeness, proportions, detail, topology, surfaces, editability, and validation so the user does not have to request them twice. Do not infer unsupported facts or a higher readiness claim than the evidence permits.

## Route by asset

- For buildings, floor plans, elevations, or measured drawings, read [references/architecture.md](references/architecture.md).
- For products, mechanisms, electronics, blades, and wiring, read [references/products.md](references/products.md).
- For people or characters, read [references/humans.md](references/humans.md).
- For animation, game-engine, AR/VR, or 3D-print delivery, also read [references/runtime-and-print.md](references/runtime-and-print.md).
- In every mode, apply [references/quality-contract.md](references/quality-contract.md).
- Before geometry, build and assess [references/evidence-pack.md](references/evidence-pack.md).
- For repository-backed generation or comparison, use [references/standard-job.md](references/standard-job.md); do not create an ad-hoc TypeScript/JavaScript generator entrypoint.
- Routing is additive for mixed subjects. Apply the primary reference and every attached, embedded, worn, or held subasset reference that affects the result.

## Shared workflow

1. Convert the request and evidence into an internal completion contract before modeling. For repo-backed work, emit one bounded `morphloom.job/0.1` JSON file and run the standard inspect/build commands; the engine—not agent-authored prose—decides whether the result is a review draft or delivery candidate. Assess the Evidence Pack by resolved capabilities—shape, depth, scale, surface, interfaces, and domain-specific structure—not by counting photographs. A single dimensioned drawing may resolve several capabilities; many redundant photos may still fail. When use is unspecified, target a semi-professional high-detail editable review asset—not a low-detail preview.
2. Identify each source by view and evidence strength. Resolve rotation, coordinate axes, anatomical/screen-side mapping, and which facade or datum each projection extends from before assigning coordinates.
3. Build and lock a `morphloom.fidelity/0.2` contract before geometry: calibrated source cameras, a strict detail inventory, signature features, material regions, per-feature thresholds, proof views, iteration limits, and token budget. An orthographic source needs at least four named 3D↔2D anchors; a perspective source needs at least six non-coplanar anchors. Store each anchor's coordinates and evidence reference, recompute the camera receipt, require positive projective depth, and block more than 4 px RMS reprojection error. A reported count or error without reproducible anchors is not evidence. Every recognizable or functional feature needs a stable ASCII id, evidence status, treatment, and proof view. Every other visible feature must still resolve to geometry, material zone, decal, normal detail, inference, or an explicit evidence-based exclusion; “not signature-defining” is not an exclusion reason.
4. Convert structure into semantic edit units. Preserve holes, gaps, courtyards, seams, openings, connectors, and other negative space as geometry—not texture or prose. Use measured or manufacturer dimensions where available and mark unsupported depth or hidden construction `estimated` or `inferred`. For a drawing-based building, attach a source-derived `planFootprint` contract and ray-project the compiled floor carriers from above; a metadata flag or matching envelope cannot substitute for occupied-region IoU, overbuild/underbuild, and protected-void checks. In every domain, attach `dimensionContracts` for every delivery-relevant measured or datasheet dimension, and re-measure the compiled assembly or named component in world-space X/Y/Z as size/min/max/center; for a rotated blade, raked member, bracket, or other oriented part, use `space: component-local` with `measurement: size` so world-AABB inflation cannot replace the true length, width, or thickness. The shared readiness gate must catch wrong product envelopes, member sizes, storeys, openings, sills, parapets, and datums even when other projections are unchanged. For hole, pin, fastener, lens, connector, hinge, or mating-interface pitch, add stable component-local `dimensionAnchors` inside the compiled part bounds and lock their transformed axis or spatial distance with an `anchorPair` contract; when both anchors belong to the same rotated carrier, use `space: component-local` to audit pitch along the carrier's own axis. Never accept matching metadata when the final datum moved, and never promote estimated image scale or an assumed dimension into this blocking contract. When at least two compatible orthographic silhouettes exist, use the bounded `visualHull` AssemblyIR operation as a form constraint before detail work; keep its unconstrained axes and concavity limitation in the evidence record. Reproject the carved volume into every supplied mask and require both minimum-view and confidence-weighted silhouette agreement; a watertight mesh with poor source-view agreement remains a blocked review draft. Use bounded `implicitSurface` graphs for evidence-supported organic blends or recesses that primitive assembly cannot express, and require padded bounds, a triangle budget, and manifold output after at most four deterministic resolution refinements.
5. Assign physical surface finishes. Include angle-dependent roughness/specular response, correctly scaled micro-normal detail, clearcoat/transmission/IOR where applicable, and anisotropy for directional surfaces. For a photographic plate used as PBR base colour, remove only broad baked illumination in linear light with bounded correction; preserve global exposure, chroma, markings, and fine/medium structure, record correction metrics, and cap confidence because one image cannot recover true albedo. Geometry height, normal response, and roughness must come from the same admitted, de-lit linear-light pixels; do not mix raw lit geometry evidence with corrected PBR evidence. Partition every reference-conditioned mesh by source-facing triangle: project the plate only where the signed face normal points toward the admitted source, retain authored UVs on tangent/rear faces, and mark those faces unobserved instead of repeating or mirroring the photograph. For irregular granular or porous references, preserve fine, medium, and coarse surface bands instead of collapsing the image into one high-pass noise scale. Run `npm run surface:prepare` and reject its output when `referenceSurfaceAudit.pass` is false; a nominal material label or attractive render cannot override weak signal, periodic structure, unstable normal response, or insufficient roughness variation. Fingerprint the actual pixel payload and glTF-visible sampler state of every colour, normal, roughness, metalness, emissive, AO, alpha, or displacement texture; do not confuse GPU upload alignment or an unrefreshed derived matrix with interchange drift. Fail closed on uninspectable pixels or sampler properties glTF cannot serialize, including unsupported mapping/format/type, texture anisotropy, premultiplied alpha, transform centre/manual matrix, or invalid mip-filter state. Also fingerprint glTF-visible opacity, emission strength, normal strength, clearcoat, transmission/volume, IOR, specular, sheen, iridescence, and anisotropy values, and compare those semantics after reopening the delivered GLB. A matching material name, scalar subset, resolution, or URL is not surface preservation.
6. Compile and run the locked pass sequence from [references/quality-contract.md](references/quality-contract.md). Compare the same source view first, then exterior-completeness views and the Clay, grazing-light, Wire, or X-Ray diagnostics needed to prove the asset. Capture the WebGL canvas without UI, floor, fog, bounds, measurement, or dimension helpers; use transparent output when supported so a pale asset cannot disappear into background segmentation. Use the exact same reference pixels, calibrated camera, resolution, feature ids, and feature rectangles for every candidate. Require one independent structural scene SHA-256 per candidate and preserve it across every calibrated view; bind every PNG to a unique browser capture receipt containing the locked input, scene, camera, reference, render, renderer version, canvas, and render-settings fingerprints; reject a scene that changes between views, duplicated render pixels, a screenshot hash relabelled as scene evidence, a reused or mismatched receipt, or evidence escaping the manifest directory. Use foreground-normalized height bands to expose internal-feature omissions that silhouette IoU cannot see, and compare each material region for colour, luminance, multi-scale structure, gradient direction, periodicity, irregularity, and the spatial placement of local texture/highlight structure. Allow only a bounded one-cell local correspondence as a minority term so curved-surface raster drift is tolerated but translated or shuffled texture cannot pass. For sparse assemblies, associate unordered endpoints of rods, cables, legs, antennae, and supports across calibrated orthographic views, solve bounded 3D landmark positions, and keep the edit only when the worst gate, Pareto frontier, or bounded sparse hybrid improves without material per-gate regression. Fit the primary body separately, retain candidate/rollback receipts, and block underconstrained azimuths or excessive reprojection error. Check direction and adjacency as well as feature counts. Never advance on an average when any critical feature, material region, or calibrated view fails its own threshold. Automatic scores may report a lead, but superiority requires the domain's minimum distinct views plus a balanced blind panel of at least five unique raters.
7. Treat `buildReady` only as permission to compile a review draft. Treat the first compiling draft as a checkpoint, not a deliverable. Fix the highest-impact failed feature or relationship in the IR, rebuild, and repeat until the Evidence Pack is `deliveryReady` and every compiled-asset blocker passes. Revert a regressing correction, refine the contract when the same defect survives twice, and stop for evidence when improvement plateaus or the declared iteration/token ceiling is reached. If a capability remains unresolved, request the property—not a prescribed number or type of files.
8. Deliver editable GLB plus source IR, comparison renders, and an evidence-aware audit. Apply every natural-language revision as a fingerprint-bound named-component patch; reject stale fingerprints, missing targets, empty edits, unsafe transforms, or any patch that changes an unrequested component. Preserve the input/output and unaffected-component fingerprints as the iterative-edit receipt, then rerun only dependent gates before the full release chain. Validate the exact GLB bytes with Khronos glTF Validator, an independent parser, and the runtime reopener; validator success cannot substitute for skeleton/weight checks, a game budget, print units/features, or an actual target-application import. For a Blender target, run a clean import→edit one named component→export→clean reimport plus a second no-op stability round trip. Compare names, requested transform, non-target bounds, polygon/material-slot counts, and seam re-index diagnostics; export tangents whenever normal maps are present and require zero Khronos errors or warnings on both Blender-produced files. State only the remaining survey, multi-view, bench-test, DCC/engine import, rigging, slicing, or artist work that materially affects the requested use.

Prefer correcting source interpretation and IR over hiding a mismatch with camera angle, material, fog, part count, triangle count, or a high quality number. Never report success while an obvious source-defining mismatch remains visible.
